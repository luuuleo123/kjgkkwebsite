Last Updated: August 9, 2026

1. Introduction

苏州杨芸电子商务有限公司 ("we", "us", "our", or "AdOptimizer") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information of Amazon Selling Partners who use our platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your data when you access and use AdOptimizer.

By using AdOptimizer, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use our services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information to provide our services:

  • Account Information: Your name, email address, company name, and contact details provided during registration.
  • Amazon API Data: Data retrieved through Amazon SP-API and Amazon Ads API, including but not limited to product listings, inventory levels, order details, pricing information, advertising campaign data, sales reports, and financial metrics. This data is accessed only with your explicit OAuth authorization.
  • Usage Data: Log data, IP addresses, browser type, device information, and interaction patterns with our platform.
  • Communication Data: Records of your correspondence with our support team.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for the following purposes:

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the AdOptimizer platform and its features.
  • To process and analyze your Amazon seller data for inventory management, pricing optimization, advertising automation, and business analytics.
  • To communicate with you regarding your account, service updates, and support requests.
  • To improve our platform, develop new features, and ensure security and compliance.
  • To comply with legal obligations and Amazon's Acceptable Use Policy.

4. Data Storage and Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • All data is stored on secure cloud servers with encryption at rest and in transit (TLS 1.2+).
  • Access to Amazon API credentials and seller data is restricted to authorized personnel only.
  • We conduct regular security audits and vulnerability assessments.
  • We do not store your Amazon Seller Central login credentials. Authentication is handled exclusively through Amazon's OAuth 2.0 flow.

Important: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or Amazon seller data to third parties for marketing purposes.

5. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We may share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: With trusted cloud hosting and infrastructure providers who assist in operating our platform, bound by strict confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal Compliance: When required by law, regulation, or valid legal process, or to protect our rights, property, or safety.
  • Amazon Compliance: As necessary to comply with Amazon's SP-API and Ads API policies, including responding to audits or investigations.

6. Amazon Data Handling

As an Amazon Public Solution Provider, we adhere to Amazon's Data Protection Policy and Acceptable Use Policy:

  • We access Amazon seller data only through authorized SP-API and Ads API endpoints.
  • We use Amazon data solely to provide services directly to the authorized Selling Partner.
  • We do not aggregate, anonymize, or use Amazon data for purposes unrelated to the services requested by the seller.
  • Upon your request or account termination, we will delete your data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • Portability: Request transfer of your data to another service provider.
  • Withdrawal of Consent: Revoke your OAuth authorization at any time via Amazon Seller Central, which will immediately cease our access to your Amazon data.

8. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to enhance your experience, analyze usage patterns, and maintain session security. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings.

9. Children's Privacy

AdOptimizer is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
