Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 9, 2026

1. Introduction

AdOptimizer ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your data when you use our platform. We integrate with Amazon's official APIs — Amazon Ads API and Selling Partner API (SP-API) — to provide seller automation services.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect data necessary to provide our services, and only with your explicit OAuth 2.0 authorization. We do not store your Amazon seller account password.

2.1 Amazon Ads API Data

  • Campaign data (Sponsored Products, Brands, Display)
  • Keywords, bids, budgets, and targeting settings
  • Advertising performance metrics (impressions, clicks, conversions, ACOS, ROAS)
  • Advertising reports and analytics data

2.2 Selling Partner API (SP-API) Data

  • Product Information: Product titles, descriptions, images, ASINs, SKUs, variant details, A+ content, and catalog attributes
  • Inventory Data: FBA stock levels, in-transit quantities, storage limits, inventory health status, and warehouse locations
  • Order Data: Order IDs, order status, shipping addresses, tracking numbers, and fulfillment details
  • Pricing Data: Current product prices, competitor pricing information, and pricing rule configurations
  • Business Reports: Sales reports, financial settlement reports, inventory reports, returns reports, and tax documents
Important: We do not collect or store sensitive buyer personal information such as full names, phone numbers, or email addresses. Any buyer data accessed through Orders API is used solely for order fulfillment tracking and is handled in accordance with Amazon's Data Protection Policy.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used solely to provide and improve our services:

3.1 Advertising Optimization (Ads API)

  • Automated bidding adjustments and budget allocation
  • Keyword management, expansion, and negative keyword automation
  • Performance reporting, ACOS/ROAS tracking, and campaign analytics

3.2 Seller Operations (SP-API)

  • Product catalog synchronization and listing quality management
  • Inventory monitoring, replenishment alerts, and FBA inbound planning
  • Order tracking, shipping status updates, and logistics coordination
  • Dynamic pricing adjustments based on market competition and profit margins
  • Automated report generation and business intelligence dashboards

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • Encryption in Transit: All data transmitted via HTTPS using TLS 1.2+
  • Encryption at Rest: All stored data is encrypted using AES-256
  • Access Controls: Role-based access control (RBAC) with multi-factor authentication
  • Network Security: Firewalls, intrusion detection, and DDoS protection
  • Regular Audits: Security assessments and vulnerability scans

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third parties.

Your data is only accessible to authorized personnel within our organization, strictly for the purpose of providing our services. We do not use your data for advertising, marketing, or any purpose unrelated to the services you have subscribed to.

6. Data Retention

  • We retain your data only for as long as necessary to provide our services
  • Advertising performance data is retained for up to 24 months for historical analytics
  • Order and inventory data is retained for up to 12 months
  • You may request deletion of your data at any time (see Section 8)

7. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access: Request a copy of all data we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Deletion: Request complete deletion of your data ("Right to be Forgotten")
  • Portability: Request export of your data in a machine-readable format
  • Revocation: Revoke OAuth authorization at any time through your Amazon Seller Central account, which will immediately cease all data access

8. Data Deletion Request

To request deletion of your data, please email us at leo@kjgkk.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request". We will process your request within 30 days and confirm completion via email.

9. Compliance

We comply with:

  • Amazon's Data Protection Policy and Acceptable Use Policy
  • Amazon Ads API Terms of Use
  • Amazon Selling Partner API Terms of Use
  • General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) where applicable
  • California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) where applicable

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes via email or through our platform at least 30 days before they take effect.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us:
