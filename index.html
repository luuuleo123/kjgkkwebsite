About AdOptimizer

AdOptimizer is a public solution provider application developed and operated by 苏州杨芸电子商务有限公司 (Suzhou Yangyun E-Commerce Co., Ltd.). The platform is built exclusively for Amazon Selling Partners, integrating official Amazon Selling Partner API (SP-API) and Amazon Ads API to deliver automated operations across product management, inventory monitoring, order fulfillment, pricing strategy, advertising optimization, and business analytics.

Our mission is to help Amazon sellers reduce manual workload, improve advertising ROI, and make data-driven decisions through secure, compliant API-based automation.

SP-API Powered Operations

Core features aligned with requested SP-API roles

📦

Product Information Management

Via Catalog Items API and Listings API, batch retrieve and update product details, A+ content, images, and variant information. Ensure listing accuracy and consistency across all SKUs.

SP-API · Catalog & Listings
📊

Intelligent Inventory Monitoring

Via FBA Inventory API and Fulfillment Inbound API, monitor real-time stock levels, in-transit quantities, and storage limits. Auto-trigger restock alerts to prevent stockouts and overage fees.

SP-API · Inventory & FBA
🚚

Order & Logistics Tracking

Via Orders API, retrieve order details, shipping status, and logistics tracking in real time. Auto-generate shipping notifications and after-sales follow-up workflows.

SP-API · Orders & Shipping
💰

Dynamic Pricing Strategy

Via Product Pricing API, track competitor prices and market trends continuously. Adjust prices automatically based on preset rules to maintain Buy Box competitiveness and profit margins.

SP-API · Pricing
📈

Operations Data Analytics

Via Reports API, schedule automated downloads of sales, inventory, and financial reports. Generate interactive dashboards to support data-driven inventory and pricing decisions.

SP-API · Reports & Analytics

Amazon Ads API Solutions
